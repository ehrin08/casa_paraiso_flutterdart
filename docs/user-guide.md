# User Guide

## First launch

Review or skip the three introduction pages. They explain service discovery, appointment management, and local storage. The introduction can be replayed from Profile.

## Browse services

Use Home for featured packages and spa contact information. Open Services to compare all four packages. Select a package to review its duration, price, massage styles, treatment choices, and paid extras.

## Make an appointment

1. Select one massage style.
2. Select one included treatment when required.
3. Add the Back Massage or VIP Room if desired.
4. Choose a business date and one available 30-minute start interval.
5. Enter a full name and Philippine mobile number. Email and notes are optional.
6. Accept local-data consent on the first booking.
7. Review the complete appointment and pay-at-spa notice.
8. Confirm and retain the displayed reference code.

The booking is saved only in the current app installation or browser. Contact Casa Paraiso directly when real-world confirmation is required.

## Manage appointments

Bookings groups records into Upcoming, Completed, and Cancelled. Open an upcoming appointment to reschedule its date/time or cancel it before the scheduled start. Rescheduling retains all service selections and the original reference.

## Profile and settings

Profile allows editing saved customer details, switching between English and Filipino, reviewing reminder behavior, opening official contact actions, replaying onboarding, and resetting all local data.

## Contact

Use Call us to choose DITO, TM, or landline. Message us opens Facebook Messenger. Directions opens the supplied Google Maps search for Casa Paraiso Body & Wellness Spa in Cuta East, Santa Teresita, Batangas.
